Sahand Tabatabaei

I am a quantum control and sensing researcher working across theory, numerical modeling, and experiment. I develop experimentally realistic quantum control and sensing protocols, taking them from analytical modeling and simulation to waveform generation, hardware integration, measurement, and interpretation.

My work has focused primarily on nanoscale spin systems, where I have developed and tested protocols for extending coherence, detecting weak magnetic fields, and probing many-body spin dynamics. My broader interests are in quantum control, sensing, and Hamiltonian engineering across diverse hardware platforms.
